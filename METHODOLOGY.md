# Methodology

This repository presents a public X thread between @burnmydays and @grok that ran from December 26, 2025 through January 7, 2026. It is not a private chat export, a prompt-engineering transcript, or a controlled lab benchmark. It is a public, timestamped interaction in which the Grok X account engaged the MO§ES™ constitutional governance framework cold.

## What It Is

- A 339-exchange public thread on X.
- A recovered archive with 216 Grok responses matched where possible.
- A curated presentation of the thread as a governance stress test.
- A provenance-preserving record: tweet IDs remain attached to the exchange record.

## What It Is Not

- Not a raw chatlog dump.
- Not a cherry-picked highlight reel.
- Not evidence of prior private seeding.
- Not sourced from the wrong original-workspace identity file; the context files were verified against the local reference originals and the thread evidence.

## Recovery Process

The assembled thread in [thread/FULL_THREAD.md](thread/FULL_THREAD.md) combines the full operator-side record with recovered Grok responses from [evidence/raw_archive.md](evidence/raw_archive.md) and [evidence/raw_responses.md](evidence/raw_responses.md). Coverage is tracked in [thread/RECOVERY_STATUS.md](thread/RECOVERY_STATUS.md).

Known corrections are applied in the public assembled thread:

- Exchange 217 uses the truth-stagnation response, `tweet-id: 2008688647034343931`.
- The Jan 7 follow-up response, `tweet-id: 2009029783619772869`, is retained in the record.
- The capstone truth-seeking response is restored from the operator-provided X record.

## No-Seeding Position

The relevant distinction is between the Grok X account and any separate Grok LLM sessions. This record concerns the public @grok account. The thread presents MO§ES™ governance concepts to that public account in-thread; the account's responses are treated as cold engagement unless a specific exchange proves otherwise.
